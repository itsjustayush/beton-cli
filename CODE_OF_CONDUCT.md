# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BETON is a local-first project built for people who want dependable computer actions with clear boundaries. We are committed to making participation welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, operating system, or preferred workflow.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate with patience and technical curiosity. Give feedback about ideas and implementations rather than people. Welcome responsible questions, explain project conventions clearly, respect different levels of familiarity, and acknowledge useful contributions. When discussing commands that interact with a user’s machine, prioritize safety, privacy, and reproducible evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publication of private information, and threats are not acceptable. Do not submit malicious code, intentionally expose secrets or personal data, or use issue and pull-request channels to promote unsafe instructions. Security vulnerabilities must be reported privately according to `SECURITY.md`.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the project. Decisions will be made with care, consistency, and respect for the people involved.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers at [info.cometlabs@gmail.com](mailto:info.cometlabs@gmail.com) with the relevant context and links. Please do not include credentials, private keys, tokens, or other sensitive data. Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the private security channel described in `SECURITY.md`, not through a public issue or conduct report.
